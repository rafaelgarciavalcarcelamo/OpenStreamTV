# Security and privacy

La app no incluye canales ni contenido. Usa fuentes autorizadas; no pegues URLs privadas ni credenciales en issues; revisa usesCleartextTraffic y prefiere HTTPS; los diagnósticos no deben guardar secretos.

## Before publishing

- Search the diff for secrets and personal data.
- Review permissions, URLs, and generated artifacts.
- Confirm logs contain no tokens or passwords.
- Test with non-production accounts, devices, and tenants.
